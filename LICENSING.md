External contributions are accepted only under the [Sonemory Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Contributors keep ownership of their contributions but grant the project owner the broad copyright and patent rights needed to:

- keep the contribution available in the AGPL community edition;
- maintain, modify, and distribute the project;
- sublicense the contribution under commercial or proprietary terms; and
- transfer project stewardship to a successor that accepts the CLA obligations.

This inbound grant is necessary to preserve both licensing paths as the contributor base grows.
