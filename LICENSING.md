Sonemory uses an **AGPL + commercial license + CLA** model. The paths below are alternatives; receiving the source code does not automatically grant a commercial exception.

## 1. Community license: AGPL-3.0-only

Unless a file clearly says otherwise, the source code in this repository is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only** (`AGPL-3.0-only`). The complete, controlling text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

You may use Sonemory commercially under the AGPL. You must still comply with all AGPL conditions. In particular, section 13 requires an operator of a modified version that users interact with remotely through a computer network to offer those users the Corresponding Source of that modified version.

The `-only` choice means this repository does not automatically authorize use under a future AGPL version.

For deployed web versions, the product interface should provide a prominent license and source link that identifies the source corresponding to the running release. A link to a moving branch is not a reliable substitute for a version-matched tag or source archive.

## 2. Separate commercial license

If an organization wants to distribute, embed, modify, or provide Sonemory without satisfying the AGPL obligations, it must obtain a separate written commercial license from the project owner before that use begins.

Commercial terms are negotiated separately and are not included in this public repository. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). Until a commercial agreement is signed by the project owner, the public code is available only under the AGPL path above.

## 3. Contributions and CLA

External contributions are accepted only under the [Sonemory Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) or a signed [Entity Contributor License Agreement](docs/legal/ENTITY-CLA-TEMPLATE.md). Contributors keep ownership of their contributions but grant the project owner the broad copyright and patent rights needed to:

- keep the contribution available in the AGPL community edition;
- maintain, modify, and distribute the project;
- sublicense the contribution under commercial or proprietary terms; and
- transfer project stewardship to a successor that accepts the CLA obligations.

This inbound grant is necessary to preserve both licensing paths as the contributor base grows.

CLA acceptance must be recorded according to [docs/cla-operations.md](docs/cla-operations.md). A checkbox cannot cure missing employer authority or unclear ownership.

## 4. Content, dependencies, and brand

- User-imported textbooks, word lists, audio, and other learning material are not relicensed merely because Sonemory processes them.
- Third-party components or assets remain under their own notices and licenses. The current inventory and admission policy are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- The Sonemory name and brand assets are not licensed by the AGPL or by this overview. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

## 5. What this model does not do

- It does not prohibit commercial use under the AGPL.
- It does not turn user-imported learning content into open-source content.
- It does not guarantee that every future dependency can be commercially relicensed.
- It does not grant Sonemory trademark or “official product” rights.
- It does not replace privacy, child-safety, education, consumer, accessibility, security, tax, or export compliance.
- It does not let the Project Owner withdraw AGPL rights already granted for a released version.

## 6. Operational guidance

The decision history, scenario matrix, business tradeoffs, release checklist, and unresolved owner decisions are documented in [docs/licensing-strategy.md](docs/licensing-strategy.md).

Before the first external contribution or commercial contract, the Project Owner should publish or insert the full legal name and notice details of the contracting party. Organization Contributions require a separately completed and signed Entity CLA.

## 7. Precedence

For the community license, the text of [LICENSE](LICENSE) controls over summaries in this repository. A signed commercial agreement controls only for the customer and scope identified in that agreement. This overview is not a substitute for legal advice.
