# Sonemory commercial licensing

Sonemory's public source code is available under `AGPL-3.0-only`. Commercial use that complies with the AGPL is allowed and does not require a separate fee.

A separate commercial license is intended for organizations that need different terms, including situations such as:

- distributing Sonemory in a closed-source product;
- offering a modified hosted service without providing the AGPL Corresponding Source to its users;
- embedding Sonemory in proprietary software or hardware where AGPL compliance is not suitable; or
- obtaining negotiated warranty, support, indemnity, branding, or deployment terms.

Buying a commercial license is not required merely because a user is a company, charges money, or earns revenue. It is required when the intended use is outside the AGPL path and the customer needs additional rights from the Project Owner.

## No license is granted by this file

This document is only a licensing notice. It is **not** a commercial license, quotation, promise of availability, or permission to avoid the AGPL. A commercial license becomes effective only through a separate written agreement signed by the project owner and the customer.

Until that agreement is signed, use of the public repository remains governed by the AGPL.

A commercial license can cover only code and assets that the Project Owner owns or is authorized to sublicense commercially. Third-party libraries, models, voices, fonts, datasets, textbooks, and user-imported content remain subject to their own terms. Review [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the current inventory.

## Requesting terms

Contact the owner of [yezi5yue/Sonemory](https://github.com/yezi5yue/Sonemory) through the GitHub profile or repository to request commercial terms. Do not post confidential business information in a public Issue.

Commercial agreements should identify the licensed version or commit, permitted products and deployments, term, territory, fees, support obligations, trademark rights, and treatment of customer modifications.

The repository includes a [commercial agreement negotiation template](docs/legal/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md). It is intentionally incomplete and does not become effective until the actual parties fill the required terms, obtain appropriate legal review, and sign it.

## Questions to resolve before quoting

- Is the customer distributing copies, operating a modified network service, embedding the code, or only using an unmodified release?
- Can the customer comply with AGPL instead of buying an exception?
- Which exact tag or commit is licensed, and does every included component permit commercial sublicensing?
- Which products, Affiliates, contractors, territories, deployments, devices, or users are covered?
- Are source delivery, upgrades, support, uptime, warranty, indemnity, privacy processing, or security obligations included?
- Does the customer need any trademark, white-label, co-branding, certification, or publicity rights?
- Who owns Customer Modifications, and may they be contributed back to the community edition?
