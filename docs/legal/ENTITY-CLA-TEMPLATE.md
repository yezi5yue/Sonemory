## 1. Definitions

**Affiliate** means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with You. **Control** means direct or indirect power to direct management, ownership of more than fifty percent of voting interests, or equivalent beneficial control.

**Authorized Contributor** means an employee or contractor listed in Schedule A, or later identified to the Project Owner in writing by an authorized representative, whom You authorize to Submit Contributions covered by this Agreement.

**Contribution** means any original work of authorship, code, documentation, design, test, data, or other material intentionally submitted by an Authorized Contributor to Sonemory for inclusion in the project. Material conspicuously marked “Not a Contribution” is excluded.

**Project Materials** means Sonemory and any work derived from or incorporating a Contribution.

**Submit** means sending a Contribution through a pull request, commit, patch, issue attachment, or another contribution channel controlled by the Project Owner.

You and Your Affiliates retain ownership of the Copyright in covered Contributions. This Agreement is a license, not a copyright assignment.

This grant includes the right to license covered Contributions under any terms, including open-source, source-available, commercial, or proprietary terms, and to offer paid exceptions or commercial licenses.

## 4. Patent license

You grant the Project Owner and recipients of the Project Materials a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license, with the right to sublicense, to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer a covered Contribution alone or in combination with the Project Materials. This license covers only patent claims owned or controlled by You or Your Affiliates that are necessarily infringed by the Contribution or its combination with the Project Materials as submitted.

## 5. Community-edition commitment

If the Project Owner includes a covered Contribution in a publicly available Sonemory community edition, the Project Owner will also make that Contribution available as part of that community edition under `AGPL-3.0-only`. This does not require proprietary versions, customer-specific changes, trademarks, or separately licensed materials to be published.

## 6. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, You will obtain from Authorized Contributors any waivers, non-assertions, or consents reasonably necessary for the uses authorized by this Agreement.

## 7. Your representations

You represent that:

1. the signatory has authority to bind You and the Affiliates identified in Schedule B;
2. You or those Affiliates own or control the rights needed to grant this Agreement;
3. Authorized Contributors are permitted to submit covered Contributions on Your behalf;
4. the grants do not breach employment, client, confidentiality, open-source, data, privacy, or other obligations;
5. third-party material and its license will be clearly identified in the relevant pull request;
6. Contributions will not contain confidential information, credentials, unlawfully collected data, or identifiable children's data; and
7. You will promptly notify the Project Owner when an Authorized Contributor loses authority, without affecting rights already granted for earlier Contributions.

## 8. No obligation and disclaimer

The Project Owner is not required to accept or use a Contribution. Except for the representations in section 7, Contributions are provided “as is,” without warranties or conditions of any kind, to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## 9. Contribution records

Each covered pull request must identify the Contributor Entity and include the individual submitter's confirmation that the submission is authorized under this Agreement. The Project Owner may reject a submission if the authorization record is incomplete or inconsistent.

## 10. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the last signature date and continues until terminated by either party in writing. Termination stops coverage for future submissions but does not revoke licenses already granted for Contributions submitted while the Agreement was active.
